diagram sequence
participant I: Initiator
participant R: Responder
note leading I: SEK from the random generator, Salt 128 bits, KEK from PBKDF2 over the passphrase
I -> R: KMREQ, the KM message with KK, cipher, lengths, salt and the wrapped key
note trailing R: derives the same KEK, unwraps, checks the integrity vector
R --> I: KMRSP, the same KM message returned
note span I R: payload encrypted with AES-CTR, the header stays in the clear
