diagram flow
start conn: Connection configuration
decision mode: Which layout?
process caller: The caller is the Initiator
process listener: The listener is the Responder
process contest: Cookie contest during the handshake
process winner: The greater cookie becomes the Initiator
process loser: The other peer becomes the Responder
end ext: Initiator sends HSREQ and KMREQ, Responder answers HSRSP and KMRSP
conn -> mode
mode -> caller: caller-listener
caller -> listener
mode -> contest: rendezvous
contest -> winner
winner -> loser
listener -> ext
loser -> ext
