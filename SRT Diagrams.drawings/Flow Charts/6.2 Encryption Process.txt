diagram flow
start pass: Pre-shared passphrase
process salt: Salt is 128 random bits, carried in the KM message
process kek: KEK is PBKDF2 over the passphrase, the low 64 bits of the salt, 2048 rounds
process sek: SEK is fresh random bits of the configured length
process wrap: Wrap the SEK with the KEK and put it in the KM message
process iv: IV is the top 112 bits of the salt, exclusive-ored with the sequence number
end out: Payload encrypted with AES-CTR, header left readable
pass -> kek
salt -> kek
kek -> wrap
sek -> wrap
salt -> iv
iv -> out
sek -> out
