diagram flow
start wave: Both peers send WAVEAHAND with Version 5 and their own cookie
process md5: Each cookie is host, port and the time to one minute, scrambled with MD5
decision equal: Cookies identical?
process wait: No connection until fresh cookies are generated, up to a minute
decision greater: Is my cookie the greater?
process initiator: I am the Initiator and I attach HSREQ
process responder: I am the Responder and I attach HSRSP
end conclusion: Exchange CONCLUSION, then AGREEMENT
wave -> md5
md5 -> equal
equal -> wait: yes
equal -> greater: no
greater -> initiator: yes
greater -> responder: no
initiator -> conclusion
responder -> conclusion
