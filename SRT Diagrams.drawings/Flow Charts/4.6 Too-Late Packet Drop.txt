diagram flow
start loop: Receiver buffer read loop
process next: Find i, the next available packet position
process time: Compute PktTsbpdTime for position i
decision due: Is the play time still in the future?
process wait: Nothing is due, wait
process skip: Drop every position before i and send a fake ACK
process deliver: Deliver packet i and set pos to i plus one
loop -> next
next -> time
time -> due
due -> wait: yes
wait -> next
due -> skip: no
skip -> deliver
deliver -> next
