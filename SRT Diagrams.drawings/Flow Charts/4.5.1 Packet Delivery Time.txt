diagram flow
start arrive: Data packet arrives
process base: TsbpdTimeBase, fixed at the handshake as T_NOW minus the HSREQ timestamp
process stamp: PKT_TIMESTAMP, the packet's own microsecond stamp
process delay: TsbpdDelay, the negotiated receiver latency
process drift: Drift, the tracked clock correction
end play: PktTsbpdTime is the sum of all four
arrive -> base
base -> stamp
stamp -> delay
delay -> drift
drift -> play
