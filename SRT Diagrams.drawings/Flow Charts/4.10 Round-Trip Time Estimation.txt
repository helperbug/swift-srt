diagram flow
start ack: Receiver sends a full ACK and records the time
process ackack: Sender answers ACKACK with the same number, with no processing delay
process rtt: rtt is the ACKACK arrival minus the ACK departure
process smooth: RTT becomes seven eighths of RTT plus one eighth of rtt
process var: RTTVar becomes three quarters of RTTVar plus one quarter of the difference
end report: Both ride in the next full ACK, and the sender smooths them again
ack -> ackack
ackack -> rtt
rtt -> smooth
smooth -> var
var -> report
