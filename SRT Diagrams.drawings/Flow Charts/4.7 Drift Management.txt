diagram flow
start recv: Packet received
process delta: Compare when it was expected with its timestamp
process sample: Add the difference to the sample set, sized in packets
decision enough: Enough samples to be sure?
process keep: Keep accumulating, jitter averages out
process factor: Compute the timestamp correction factor
process apply: Adjust the time base, every scheduled delivery moves with it
recv -> delta
delta -> sample
sample -> enough
enough -> keep: no
keep -> recv
enough -> factor: yes
factor -> apply
apply -> recv
