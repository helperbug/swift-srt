diagram flow
start km: KM message arrives
process read: Read the salt and the key length from it
process kek: Derive the KEK with PBKDF2 over the passphrase and the low 64 bits of the salt
decision icv: Does the integrity check vector match?
process bad: Wrong passphrase, discard the keys, every packet stays unreadable
process sek: Unwrap the SEK
process iv: Rebuild the IV from the salt and the packet sequence number
end out: Payload decrypted with AES-CTR
km -> read
read -> kek
kek -> icv
icv -> bad: no
icv -> sek: yes
sek -> iv
iv -> out
